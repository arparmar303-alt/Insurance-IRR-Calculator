Insurance IRR Calculator

Estimate annualized return from premiums and policy benefits — works offline after saving this file.

1. Premium details

For the most reliable result, use annual premiums and annual policy-year cash flows. Non-annual frequency is approximated in the schedule.

2. Policy benefits / returns

Add every amount you expect to receive and the policy year in which it is paid. Include maturity, survival benefits, bonuses or other payouts as applicable.

3. Recurring income / cash flows

Use this for regular yearly income, such as survival benefits: enter the first year, last year, and amount received each year. Both end years are included.

How to use

  1. Enter the premium amount and number of premium payments.
  2. Add each benefit amount and the year it is received.
  3. Tap Calculate IRR to see the estimated annualized return and cash-flow schedule.

Important: This is an estimate based only on the cash flows entered. It is not an insurer quote or a guarantee. Actual returns can differ due to dates, taxes, charges, bonuses and policy terms. For policies with irregular exact dates, use a date-based XIRR calculation.
